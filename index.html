I began in technology and development at Cubix. I sat close to engineers, watched products take shape, and learned to translate technical complexity into plain, human language. I also led a team of writers, which taught me that great content is a team sport.

Chapter two

SMB Services: from a blank page to domain rating 26

I joined as a Content Writer and grew into Senior Content Specialist. I wrote the content for the entire website, from the ground up. The domain started at a rating of 0.1, practically invisible. Through search-led strategy, topic clusters, shareable infographics and content people wanted to pass on, it climbed to 26.

Finance and accounting aren't famous for being exciting. That was the fun of it: making fractional CFO, bookkeeping, tax and AI-driven business intelligence feel clear, human and worth sharing.
